# Code of Conduct

Tracks follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to everyone taking part in this project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you represent it.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and kind. People of every background and level of experience are welcome here.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to anyone affected, and learn from them.
- Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, personal or political attacks, and publishing anyone's private information without their permission are not acceptable.

The full text of the Covenant is the authority; this summary doesn't replace it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer, either by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/reallukedev/tracks/security/advisories/new) or by email via the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/reallukedev). Please don't report conduct problems in a public issue.

Every report will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow it. The consequences, from a private warning to a permanent ban, follow the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
